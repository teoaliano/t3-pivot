Pivot mode: four ways in

Every sketch shares the same Pivot view, now built as a layout tree. Start from a preset (Layout ▾), drag the gaps between panes to resize, use a pane's ⋯ menu to hide it or move it to any edge, and bring hidden panes back with + Pane. Panes: Pivot chat, Teammates, Teammate, Preview, Files, Diff. Clicking a card focuses that teammate; the Teammate, Preview, Files and Diff panes follow the focus unless you pin one with its picker. Wallpaper ▾ has a sample, your own image, and a dim level. Finished teammates collapse into a chip. The decisions strip above the composer answers the held decision.
