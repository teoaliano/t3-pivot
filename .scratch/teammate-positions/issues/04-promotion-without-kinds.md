# Promotion without kinds

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked-by: 01

## Question

Today a scout is promoted to a ship in place (`promote_scout`, `text/promotion.md`). With
positions, what is the equivalent? Options include reassigning the teammate to another
position in place (App breaker finds a bug, becomes Bug fixer), switching only its outcome,
or dropping promotion and dispatching a new teammate with the report attached. Decide which,
what the teammate keeps (worktree, branch, chat), and how the Pivot and the card show it.

From What stays fixed in the brief: a teammate whose position reports back may run in the
project checkout, read-only, with no worktree. Promoting it to a change needs a worktree
created first. Decide whether that is allowed and how it happens.
